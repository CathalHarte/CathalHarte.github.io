Raised by wolves

Having worked in the startup world for the best part of a decade, I've met a lot of people that believe they were born to be entrepreneurs, but in reality most are simply bred for it, told a thousand times before they're 5 that they're destined to be great.

They respond quickly, and correctly, when prompted with 'fetch': their dogged determination kicks in. They're diligent, admirably so, because they've been assured of their success.

For others, for the wild hearted, it can take time for the embers to be stoked, time to hear the roar of the fire.

Don't be afraid if the mantras haven't yet been installed in your own head. If you search you can find the fire in your own wild heart, and then fuel it with your actions.

As soon as the second sort of person does this, they discredit the first, in the same way that a wolf discredits a dog, by merely existing in its full majesty, its full splendour.

…Or at least that's what I hope for, for the shaggy underdog to turn out to be a wolf.

Some years back, at a job fair hosted by EPFL, I overheard a student ask an entrepreneur (who shall remain nameless) "how can I become an entrepreneur like you?" The entrepreneur responded with "you've either got it or you don't", being typically cagey about what "it" is. Supporting parents? A trust fund? Grandiose delusions?

The trick is a good one. I suppose it made that student feel that instead of asking for advice, he was asking for permission, and that his permission was denied (on what grounds, because of his humility maybe?)

The lesson to learn here is not to avoid talking to successful people, clearly there is much to gain from this. Just don't fall for that trick. You were never asking for permission, you were trying to learn, to further your own goal.

Successful, powerful people play tricks in the name of acquiring and maintaining power, and to make matters worse, the most intelligent of them are the most likely to be narcissistic.

Here are 48 more tricks you can expect them to play. Learn them, and stay safe out there.
