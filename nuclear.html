Nuclγscan

Total-body PET imaging · CERN spin-off technology

A next-generation total-body PET scanner designed for precision medicine and whole-body imaging at a level not previously possible. I build the software across the full stack: acquisition, reconstruction, and clinical integration, in addition to leading the risk management activities across the whole design. The same underlying technology is also being applied for real-time proton therapy QA.

Shown at PTCOG 2026

Qualγscan

Real-time QA for proton and particle therapy

Qualγscan is an evolving project in particle beam therapy dose verification. Tested first as a machine QA device, the project now sets its sights on in vivo verification, aiming to be the world's first commercial solution to this historically hard problem.
