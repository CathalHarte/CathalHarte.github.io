## LinkedIn

Most people think adventure is something you do. I think it is something you decide before you leave the house.

The complication: frame it as personal challenge, and the moment you hit flow, the "I" that wanted the achievement dissolves. Csikszentmihalyi noticed this. The question has to become bigger than you.

## X

Csikszentmihalyi noticed that overly individualistic people struggle to achieve flow. Makes sense: if your only reason to try is *you*, and flow dissolves the self, the wind drops from your sails just as you hit it. So the question can't be "can I summit Everest?" It has to be bigger than you.
