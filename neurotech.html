Brain-Spine Interface

A cortical implant decodes movement intent and wirelessly drives a spinal stimulator — restoring natural walking in patients with chronic spinal cord injury. Published in Nature, May 2023. Named Physics World Breakthrough of the Year.

I'd like to stress the substantial development effort we made to enable the system to be used at home. That's what makes the achievement all the more real for me.
